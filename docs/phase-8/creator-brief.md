# Phase 8 Creator And Organic Brief

Status: blocked until `PHASE8_CREATOR_COMPLIANCE_PASS=true`.

## Allowed Creator Prompt

Creators may show how they use OnSkin to organize a routine or check product-order conflicts in their own shelf. They must use real app screens and avoid medical or outcome claims.

## Required Disclosure

Use clear disclosure near the endorsement:

- `Ad` when paid, gifted, commissioned, or otherwise materially connected
- `Paid partnership with OnSkin` where platform tools support it
- A plain-language caption disclosure before any link or CTA

Do not hide disclosure in a profile bio, link page, comment, or end screen only.

## Allowed Claims

- "I use OnSkin to organize my skincare routine."
- "It helps me keep my shelf and AM/PM steps in one place."
- "This card shows a reviewed product-order conflict."
- "Progress photos are for my own comparison, without scores."

## Blocked Claims

- Treats, cures, prevents, heals, detects, or diagnoses any condition
- Clears acne, fixes rosacea, repairs barrier, reverses aging
- Dermatologist-grade, clinically proven, AI dermatologist
- Skin score, skin age, percentage improvement
- "Guaranteed results"
- "Everyone should use this active"
- Before/after gallery framed as proof of outcome

## Link Rules

Creators get only first-party links generated from the final domain. Link payloads may include:

- Source
- Campaign
- Creator handle or opaque creator ID
- Creative variant

Links must not include:

- Skin concern
- Pregnancy status
- Product names
- Ingredient names
- Photos
- User IDs
- Free-text notes

## Review Workflow

1. Creator receives this brief and the claim boundary.
2. Creator submits draft caption and screen recording.
3. OnSkin reviews for disclosure, claims, and real-feature accuracy.
4. Approved creative receives a first-party link.
5. Analytics track source/campaign only.
6. Any review, testimonial, or rating request must not ask for positive reviews or suppress negative feedback.

## Rejection Triggers

- Missing disclosure
- Medical or outcome claim
- Screenshot of unenabled feature
- Product-pushing framed as clinical advice
- Link to external checkout
- Before/after proof framing
- Any fake review, rating gate, or testimonial manipulation
