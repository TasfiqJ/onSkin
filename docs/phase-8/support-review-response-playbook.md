# Phase 8 Support And Review Response Playbook

Status: blocked until `PHASE8_SUPPORT_RESPONSE_PASS=true`.

## Support SLA

- Billing or account access: first response within 1 business day.
- Account deletion/export: first response within 1 business day.
- Safety or medical-adjacent confusion: first response within 1 business day and escalate to legal/clinical reviewer.
- General product feedback: first response within 3 business days.

## Public Review Response Rules

Do:

- Thank the user briefly.
- Give a concrete support path.
- Acknowledge billing is managed by Apple or Google when relevant.
- Correct claim confusion calmly.
- Avoid collecting personal data in public.

Do not:

- Ask users to change ratings.
- Offer rewards for positive reviews.
- Suppress negative feedback.
- Discuss health, pregnancy, products, or photos in public replies.
- Make treatment, diagnosis, or outcome claims.

## Templates

### Billing

### Account Deletion

You can delete your account in the app from You -> Delete account. If you need help, contact support at `SUPPORT_EMAIL`. Subscription billing must be cancelled separately through Apple or Google.

### Export

You can export your data in the app from You -> Export my data. If the export fails, contact support at `SUPPORT_EMAIL` and we will help.

### Feature Confusion

Layerwell is for routine and shelf organization with general cosmetic information. It is not medical advice. Please contact support at `SUPPORT_EMAIL` if something in the app was unclear.
