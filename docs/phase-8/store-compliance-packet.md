# Phase 8 Store Compliance Packet

This packet is the pre-submission checklist for App Store Connect and Play Console. It is not complete until the Phase 8 strict checks pass with external evidence.

## App Store Connect

Required before submit:

- Final app name, subtitle, screenshots, preview assets
- Final bundle ID and SKU
- App Privacy nutrition labels matching actual collection, use, retention, and sharing
- Privacy policy, terms, consumer health privacy notice, support, export, deletion URLs
- In-app purchases configured and reviewed with subscription group, trial, grace, refund/support copy
- Sign in with Apple review if third-party sign-in is present
- Account deletion available in app
- Camera usage explanation matches actual barcode, label, and progress-photo use
- Reviewer notes describe no diagnosis, no treatment, no disease detection, no score, no skin age
- Test credentials and purchase test notes

## Google Play Console

Required before submit:

- Final app name, short description, full description, screenshots, feature graphic
- Final package name and signing certificate evidence
- Data safety form matching actual collection, sharing, encryption, deletion, and retention
- Health apps / health content declarations reviewed for consumer health data handling
- Account deletion URL and in-app deletion flow
- Subscription products, base plans, offers, and grace/retry settings
- App access instructions and test credentials
- Content rating questionnaire
- Target audience and ads declaration

## Privacy Inventory

Data categories to verify:

- Account identifiers and authentication provider IDs
- Email address for account/support only
- Skin profile quiz answers and routine preferences
- Product shelf metadata
- Barcode/label scan data, if used
- Progress photos, local by default
- Subscription entitlement and purchase status
- Content-free analytics events
- Crash diagnostics
- Support messages only when user contacts support

## Claim Boundary

Public store assets must not claim or imply:

- Diagnosis, treatment, cure, prevention, detection, or clinical efficacy
- AI dermatologist, AI skin analysis, skin score, skin age, hazard score
- Guaranteed outcomes or percent improvement
- Medical-device behavior
- Creator/UGC moderation capabilities that are not live

## Open Blockers

- Final brand/domain/app IDs are not filled.
- Privacy labels/Data safety forms need final legal review.
- Store screenshots need physical-device QA captures.
- App-link association files need final Team ID, package, and certificate fingerprints.
- Test credentials and billing sandbox evidence are not attached.
