# Seven-Figure Readiness Check

Date: 2026-07-04

Verdict: conditional yes. The idea is strong enough to pursue, but a seven-figure
outcome depends on trust, retention, catalog usefulness, and paid conversion,
not more feature count.

## Differentiated Wedge

The strongest wedge is not "another ingredient scanner." It is:

- use the products already on your shelf
- find conflicts and sequencing problems
- build a calm AM/PM routine
- follow it daily
- track private progress without public scores or fear-based claims

That wedge can justify payment if beta users repeatedly get value from owned
products and come back in weeks 2-6.

## Revenue Reality

At $49.99/year, $1M gross ARR requires about 20,000 active annual subscribers
before store fees, refunds, failed payments, and churn. With median subscription
app conversion rates, the app likely needs hundreds of thousands of qualified
installs unless retention and conversion are top-quartile.

This means the beta must prove:

- users add multiple real products
- first useful insight happens quickly
- users return to Today check-off
- photo/reminder loops create week-2 and week-4 retention
- trial starts are tied to clear value moments
- users understand why this is worth paying for instead of using a free scanner

## What Customers Are Most Likely To Pay For

| Priority | Paid value |
| --- | --- |
| Shelf conflict detection | Avoids irritating combinations and product waste |
| Routine sequencing | Reduces daily decision fatigue |
| Calm habit loop | Keeps people consistent without shame or fear |
| Photo baseline/timeline | Lets users see personal progress privately |
| Independent recommendations | Valuable only if visibly independent from commerce |
| Bounded Ask advisor | Useful if deterministic, grounded, and not an unsafe chatbot |

## What Will Not Create A Seven-Figure Business By Itself

- generic ingredient safety scores
- fear-based "bad product" claims
- an open AI skin score
- affiliate commerce before trust
- peer community before moderation
- widgets before the core retention loop works
- more screens without real catalog, payments, clinical review, and beta proof

## Beta Kill Criteria

Do not scale growth if any of these are true after 50-100 real users:

- fewer than half of onboarded users add real products
- most users do not receive a useful first insight
- D7 retention is weak and no cohort returns for routine check-off
- catalog miss/wrong-match complaints dominate support
- users distrust conflict guidance or ask for proof the app cannot provide
- paywall starts happen before value and refund/cancel reasons cite confusion

## Seven-Figure Path

1. Rebrand or legally clear the name.
2. Launch only the V1 loop in `docs/v1-scope-freeze.md`.
3. Make catalog, clinical review, Supabase, RevenueCat, and native camera real.
4. Run closed beta with retention and willingness-to-pay metrics.
5. Fix confusing guidance, catalog misses, and paywall timing.
6. Scale organic growth only after retention holds.

The app earns the right to be a seven-figure company when users treat it as the
trusted system of record for their shelf and routine.
