# Phase 11 Creator Disclosure Pack

Status: BLOCKED until creator program is approved. Creator traffic is not allowed before this pack is signed.

## Creator Rules

- Every material connection must be disclosed clearly.
- Creators may not make medical, diagnostic, treatment, cure, prevention, or guaranteed-result claims.
- Creators may not imply personal results unless substantiated, typical, and approved.
- Creators may not ask followers to provide private health details in comments or DMs.
- Creators may not request or reward app-store reviews.
- Any affiliate or paid link must be disclosed near the link.

## Allowed Creator Copy

- "I use this to organize my skincare routine."
- "It helps me track products on my shelf."
- "It flags reviewed routine-order conflicts."
- "It lets me compare progress photos privately."
- "This is not medical advice."

## Banned Creator Copy

- "This diagnoses your skin."
- "This treats acne."
- "This will clear your skin."
- "This is dermatologist-approved" unless a reviewed, accurate statement exists.
- "Everyone should use this before seeing a doctor."
- undisclosed ad, affiliate, or gifted relationship

## Required Evidence

- creator list
- compensation/disclosure status
- approved copy set
- banned claims acknowledgement
- support escalation path
- takedown process
- FTC/fake-review compliance review
