# Phase 11 Ring 1 Soft Launch

Status: BLOCKED until Ring 0 exits cleanly.

Ring 1 is invite-led public launch. It uses narrow traffic from the beta waitlist, founder audience, and controlled organic posts. It is not creator scale or paid acquisition.

## Entry

- Ring 0 exit approved.
- 72-hour monitoring plan is staffed.
- Support SLA coverage active.
- Store links work from target countries.
- Dashboard filters are set to Ring 1.
- Creator posts and paid traffic remain off unless explicitly approved.

## Traffic Sources

Allowed:

- beta waitlist
- founder-controlled email/social
- direct community contacts
- small organic posts with reviewed claims

Not allowed:

- paid acquisition scale
- broad creator campaign
- incentivized reviews
- unreviewed health claims
- worldwide launch by default

## 72-Hour Review

At 24, 48, and 72 hours, record:

- installs
- onboarding completion
- product add and first value
- D1 retention where available
- paywall/trial/purchase/restore
- support volume and SLA
- catalog misses/wrong matches
- privacy/deletion/export issues
- crash/ANR/release health
- store review themes
- decision: continue, hold, rollback, or no-go
