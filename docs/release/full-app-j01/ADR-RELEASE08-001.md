# ADR-RELEASE08-001 — J01 engineering priority independent of J05

Status: PROPOSED_SOURCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED. Authority: BRAIN-RELEASE-FULLAPP-J01-REBASE08-CANDIDATE-01 and product decision BRAIN-RELEASE-SCOPE-FULL-APP-J01-2026-10-24-01. This new namespaced ADR does not replace or renumber ADR-001..051.

The historical `MOD-J01.depends_on=[M3-02]` and registry execution_order9 are engineering priority, not a physical law that a spot result must exist before pores. The first public candidate is now J01 alone. Propose removing the J05 engineering-order edge in the new overlay; retain shared owner/privacy/versioned capture foundations and all real optical/rights/scientific dependencies. Original roadmap/module bytes and dependency lists remain immutable.

Early G1 optical feasibility and G2 documentary target/rights work should resolve risk before the entire app is rebuilt. No new measurement code, participant collection or phone execution is authorized by this governance task. Later J01 engineering requires separate scope and accepted M1-03→M2 foundations, secure native capture and independent G1/G2/G3/G5 evidence. G4 independently gates every change claim. If optics cannot resolve the declared target, record NO_GO for that claimed output and return for evidence-based product review; never upscale or simulate a pore result.

J05/M3-02/M3-03/M5-01/M5-02 and all other Korean outputs defer for this public version. Shared capture infrastructure may be reused after separate authorization, but no J05 output, mask/report/score/publication is admitted. Ordinary Brain source acceptance and later main promotion remain exclusive independent decisions.
