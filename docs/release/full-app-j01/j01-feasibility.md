## G1 — physical iPhone optics first

Imaging engineer + physical-device owner preregister two actually available supported-iPhone/lens/OS profiles, not guessed model coverage. Borrow existing hardware only if separately authorized; spend0. Record exact model/lens/module, focus mode, native capture API, sensor/output dimensions, crop/zoom, orientation/mirroring, codec/ISP path, exposure/ISO/shutter/white balance, flash/torch, timestamp, saved image hash and region. Read actual saved pixel dimensions; preview size, digital zoom and upscaling provide no extra resolving evidence.

Use a rights-cleared calibrated resolution target in a lab to measure resolving performance at each declared working distance; keep lab calibration distinct from phone-only self capture. Candidate close-up distances10/15/20/30cm and existing35–45cm historical hypotheses are trial conditions, not supported minimum focus distances. Record measured focus working distance and failed focus, native pixels per calibration length and target modulation/resolution; do not infer physical pore diameter on curved skin from an uncalibrated selfie scale. Define minimum resolvable detail `d_min` from independently reviewed target performance plus sampling/contrast limits. Reviewer chooses and freezes the smallest supported pore-appearance target and required native pixel span before testing; historical six-pixel hypotheses are not validation.

Test nose and each cheek separately, three repeat captures with reposition/refocus per region/condition plus a separate session. Record actual illuminance/colour conditions where instrumentation exists, window/daylight/dim-room variation, glare/shadow/motion and with/without allowed phone illumination. Missing light instrumentation is an explicit gap, not invented lux. Compare rear-main close-up and any genuinely supported macro lens separately; front/selfie convenience is another unsupported domain until measured. No compulsory external lamp/card/stand in the final self-capture claim. Broaden participants/tones/texture only after approved consent/rights, not through unconsented founder photos.

Preregister acceptance with imaging/science reviewers: declared visible target resolves above the frozen detail/contrast criterion in the promised region/profile/lighting regime, retained native pixels and repeated captures agree within the approved noise budget, and a user can capture without lab equipment. No universal numeric threshold or accuracy is asserted by this document. Reject defocus, digital-only detail, insufficient sampling, occlusion, beauty processing, glare, unsupported profile, wrong orientation or uncontrolled scene. Preserve failures. G1 NO_GO if the target cannot be resolved; useful diary photos do not prove pore measurement.

## G3 — single-scan baseline and abstention

Candidate segmentation arithmetic or unit fixtures close engineering checks only. Reviewers preregister acceptable error and coverage at each claim granularity; no generic confidence scalar or threshold chosen after seeing test data supplies accuracy. Empty support/unsupported profile/failed quality/missing evidence returns unavailable or a specific retake reason, never zero pores or a simulated report.

Qualified reviewers inspect fairness/support exclusions and cosmetic/non-diagnostic wording for the actual territory and each output. Physical QA verifies permissions, capture accessibility, background/lock/logout/delete races and honest unavailable recovery. Source tests, simulator exports and papers cannot close this gate. Single-scan positive report requires G1/G2/G3/G5 at the exact profile/region/pipeline/build; change adds G4. Count, diameter, score, reduction, diagnosis, accuracy, percentages and simulated reports remain unadmitted in this proposal.
