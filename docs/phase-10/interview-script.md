# Phase 10 Beta Interview Script

Status: TEMPLATE. Use only with testers who explicitly opted into interviews.

## Segments

Interview at least one tester from each segment before Phase 10 exit:

- activated and retained
- installed but not activated
- catalog-blocked
- paywall-blocked
- privacy-sensitive
- support-heavy
- churned

## Opening

Confirm:

- consent to interview
- permission to take notes
- no request for medical advice
- no need to share sensitive health details
- tester ID, platform, build, wave

## Questions

1. What were you trying to accomplish when you opened the app?
2. Where did you first feel the app helped?
3. Where did you first feel stuck or skeptical?
4. How did adding products compare with what you expected?
5. Did any catalog miss or wrong match reduce trust?
6. Did the routine or conflict guidance feel clear and non-medical?
7. What did you understand Pro to unlock?
8. Did the paywall appear too early, too late, or at the right time?
9. Did reminders or photos make the app more useful or more sensitive?
10. Did you know where deletion, export, and support controls were?
11. What would make you come back next week?
12. What would make you pay?
13. What would make you uninstall?
14. What would you tell a friend this app is for?
15. Should this launch publicly, launch narrowly, hold, or stop? Why?

## Close

- Ask if there is one issue the team must fix before launch.
- Ask permission for follow-up if needed.
- Do not request a public review or testimonial.
- File notes under tester ID with category tags.

## Analysis Tags

- first value
- onboarding
- catalog
- guidance trust
- privacy
- payment
- support
- retention
- willingness to pay
- launch risk
