# Phase 10 Tester Brief

Status: TEMPLATE. Send only after final brand, support address, privacy policy, terms, beta build, and support desk are live.

## What You Are Testing

You are testing a pre-release version of OnSkin focused on routine organization, product shelf setup, reviewed product-order conflict guidance, progress capture, reminders, and Pro subscription flows. The beta is meant to find confusing, broken, unsafe, or untrusted parts of the experience before public launch.

## Important Boundaries

- OnSkin is not medical advice.
- OnSkin does not diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, or detect skin conditions.
- Do not use beta feedback channels for urgent health concerns.
- Do not upload or type sensitive medical, prescription, pregnancy, or identity details unless the app explicitly asks and the policy explains why.
- Photo features are optional. You can test the app without sharing photos.
- We may ask about usability and trust. We will not ask you to disclose unnecessary health details.

## What To Try

1. Install the assigned build from TestFlight or Google Play.
2. Complete onboarding using realistic answers.
3. Add products through barcode, search, or manual entry.
4. Review any shelf conflict or routine-order guidance.
5. Complete at least one routine check-off.
6. Try photo/progress and reminder flows only if you are comfortable.
7. Open Pro/paywall screens and check whether the value is clear.
8. Test restore/manage subscription flows if you are assigned to payment QA.
9. Find account deletion and data export controls.
10. Submit feedback through the approved beta support channel.

## Payment Testing

TestFlight in-app purchases use Apple sandbox behavior and do not charge testers. Android payment testing may use license testers or real payment methods depending on the assigned cohort. You will be told before any flow that can create a real charge. Unexpected charges are P0 support issues.

## Feedback Rules

Use the support channel or survey links provided in the beta invite. Include:

- platform and device
- beta build number
- what you expected
- what happened
- screenshot only if it does not expose sensitive personal details

Do not send product lists, medical photos, prescriptions, or private health information through personal DMs.

## Privacy Controls

The beta must support:

- account deletion
- data export
- consent withdrawal where applicable
- support escalation for privacy requests

If any of those paths fail, report it as a beta blocker.

## Reviews And Testimonials

Do not leave public store reviews for beta builds. Do not post testimonial claims unless you have separate written permission and required disclosures. Honest private feedback is more useful than praise.
