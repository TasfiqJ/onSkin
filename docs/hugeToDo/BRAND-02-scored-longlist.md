## Advanced candidates

### 1. RoutineKind

Meaning: a kinder way to manage a routine. It directly supports the calm,
non-punitive promise and is immediately intelligible. It is already the
development identity, which reduces migration risk but gives it no legal
priority.

Strengths:

- clearest product story and strongest spelling/pronunciation;
- works across shelf, routine, Today, progress, community, and professional
  expansion;
- no exact public App Store title found in four checked storefronts;
- no exact CIPO result in the accessible 2026-07-12 query;
- `.com` and `.app` returned no RDAP registration record in the dated check.

Risks:

- both words are common and the routine app field is crowded;
- CIPO includes `Skin&Routine` in classes 3, 9, 35, 42, and 44 and `Routine
Review` in 42, 44, and 45;
- enforceability and registrability may be weaker than for a coined word;
- `Kind` means “child” in German and Dutch, an ambiguity requiring launch-market
  linguistic review, though no offensive meaning was identified here.

### 2. Ritunera

Construction: a coined blend evoking “routine” and “new era.” Proposed
pronunciation: `rih-too-NEH-ruh`.

Strengths:

- most distinctive candidate in this list;
- no exact Apple title, exact CIPO result, `ritun*` CIPO result, or RDAP record
  for the checked `.com` and `.app` domains was found;
- broad enough for non-skincare expansion.

Risks:

- spelling and pronunciation require teaching;
- the product category is not self-evident without a subtitle;
- meaning and pronunciation need native-speaker review in every approved launch
  country;
- no USPTO, WIPO, or EUIPO result grid was available in this environment.

### 3. Rituvia

Construction: a coined name that can suggest “routine via a clearer path.”
Proposed pronunciation: `rih-TOO-vee-uh`.

Strengths:

- compact, ownable-feeling, and expansion-friendly;
- no exact Apple title or exact/near-stem CIPO result was found in the checked
  queries;
- `.app` returned no RDAP registration record.

Risks:

- `rituvia.com` is registered and was offered on a parked sales page for USD
  $500 on the check date; no purchase is authorized;
- can sound pharmaceutical, which is undesirable for a non-medical product;
- the crowded `RITU*`/`RITUAL` field includes class 9 and 42 marks and must be
  assessed by counsel.

## Holds

- `Ritualoom`: memorable but combines a crowded `RITUAL` field with “loom,”
  which can sound mystical or threatening and weakens the evidence-aware voice.
- `Shelfkind`: strong immediate fit, but `shelfkind.com` is registered, and CIPO
  includes `EVERSHELF` in classes 9/42 and `Shelf Health` in 9/35/44.
- `CalmCabinet`: clear but too descriptive, too storage-specific, and difficult
  to defend as the product expands.

## Scoring caveat

These numbers organize product judgment; they do not predict registration,
infringement, App Review, domain reservation, or commercial success. Founder
preference, customer testing, native-speaker review, and formal counsel
clearance remain separate gates.
