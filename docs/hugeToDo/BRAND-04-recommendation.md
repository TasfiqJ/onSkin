# BRAND-04 Name Recommendation

Date: 2026-07-12 (America/Toronto)
Decision status: provisional product recommendation; pending founder approval,
formal clearance, customer/linguistic validation, and reservation

## Recommended winner: RoutineKind

Recommend `RoutineKind` as the first candidate sent to counsel, not as a cleared
or production-ready identity.

It wins the product decision because it communicates the promise without a
medical or AI claim: a calmer, kinder way to use a skincare routine. It is easy
to say, hear, and spell; supports the shelf -> insight -> routine -> Today loop;
and stretches naturally to `RoutineKind Pro`, community, creator, and future
professional workflows. It is also the existing development identity, reducing
avoidable migration and release risk if it clears.

The recommendation is conditional because `Routine` is crowded and both words
are ordinary. Accessible CIPO results include `Skin&Routine` in the same core
classes and `Routine Review` in software/beauty-adjacent classes. Formal counsel
may decide the compound is weak, unavailable, difficult to enforce, or too
close to an earlier mark. That outcome must be accepted without trying to solve
it with a subtitle or logo.

## Backup 1: Ritunera

Use `Ritunera` if counsel rejects or materially limits `RoutineKind` and founder
testing accepts a coined name.

Why it is first backup:

- strongest inherent distinctiveness in the shortlist;
- no exact Apple or accessible CIPO result found;
- no `ritun*` CIPO result found;
- no `.com` or `.app` RDAP record returned in the point-in-time check;
- supports a broader company/product family.

Residual risks:

- listeners may not know the spelling or stress;
- no category meaning without the subtitle;
- native-speaker and confusion searches are incomplete;
- USPTO/WIPO/EUIPO result grids remain unverified.

Proposed pronunciation: `rih-too-NEH-ruh`.

## Backup 2: Rituvia

Use `Rituvia` only after counsel evaluates the `RITU*` field and founder accepts
the domain and pharmaceutical-sound tradeoffs.

Why it remains a backup:

- distinctive, compact, and expansion-friendly;
- no exact Apple or accessible CIPO result found;
- `.app` returned no RDAP registration record.

Residual risks:

- `rituvia.com` is registered and was offered on a parked sales page;
- spelling and pronunciation require teaching;
- it can sound like a drug name in a health-adjacent context;
- CIPO contains `RITUAL` marks in classes 9 and 42 and other `RITU-` forms in
  medical/pharmaceutical classes.

Proposed pronunciation: `rih-TOO-vee-uh`.

## Why the alternatives lose

- `Ritualoom` is more mystical, contains a crowded root, and “loom” has a
  negative English sense.
- `Shelfkind` narrows the platform to inventory and has domain plus same-class
  shelf-formative adjacency.
- `CalmCabinet` is clear but descriptive and difficult to expand or defend.
- `OnSkin` is an exact same-category incumbent and remains rejected.

## Decision rule

1. Send all three finalists to counsel together so later-filed backup work does
   not restart the process.
2. If counsel gives strong written clearance to `RoutineKind`, run founder and
   customer pronunciation/memory tests, then reserve authorized assets.
3. If counsel rejects or materially limits it, choose between `Ritunera` and
   `Rituvia` based on counsel risk first, then product testing and domain cost.
4. Do not attempt to “fix” an adverse clearance result with a logo, subtitle,
   punctuation, or a generic modifier without a new written opinion.
5. Freeze the identity registry and begin BRAND-07 through BRAND-10 only after
   the exact mark and conditions are approved and reservation evidence exists.

## Explicit non-conclusions

This recommendation does not state that any name is legally available,
registrable, non-infringing, globally safe, accepted by Apple, commercially
successful, or reservable. No filing, purchase, account creation, or identity
change was performed.
