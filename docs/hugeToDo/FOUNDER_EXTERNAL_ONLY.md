# List 2 — Actions ChatGPT Cannot Perform by Itself

These are the only V1 actions that require the founder, a licensed/credentialed human, a physical iPhone, Apple, or a vendor. Do not send secrets, raw MFA codes, private keys, full bank/tax records, or passwords through chat.

## H-01 — Apple legal/account/financial enrollment

You must personally control the Apple Account and complete the parts Apple requires from a real account holder:

- Apple Developer membership enrollment/payment;
- identity/MFA/recovery challenges;
- truthful individual vs organization seller choice and legal entity data;
- Program License / App Store Connect agreements;
- tax and banking forms/status;
- any Apple payment/identity challenge.

What to give back: screenshots or copied status text with private numbers/addresses/account identifiers redacted. Useful fields: membership active/expiry, Team ID if safe to share, Account Holder/Admin role, agreements active/pending, tax/banking status complete/pending.

## H-02 — Final name/trademark decision and purchase authorization

You need qualified trademark counsel to clear the exact final app name in the real launch territory/classes. You then choose/approve that exact spelling and authorize/pay for domain and any filing/reservation.

What to give back: counsel's written decision/conditions, chosen exact name, countries/classes, and redacted reservation/domain receipt. Do not claim a web knockout search alone is legal clearance.

## H-03 — Professional release review

Qualified people must make the judgments automation cannot self-sign:

- privacy/consumer-health/legal/regulatory claims review;
- dermatologist review of exact conflict/routine/cycle user guidance;
- cosmetic chemist review of exact compatibility/application-order corpus;
- IP/content-rights review where the exact V1 still needs it;
- export/encryption classification advice if the release facts require a professional determination.

What I can do before/after: prepare packets, source hashes, reviewer questions, diff requested changes, implement corrections, and generate detached signoff records. What you return is the reviewer identity/credentials, dated decision, exact reviewed hash/build and conditions.

## H-04 — Minimum production accounts and paid services

You must create/approve/pay and complete identity/MFA/terms challenges for the minimum selected stack:

- Expo/EAS;
- Supabase staging + production;
- RevenueCat;
- transactional SMTP/email delivery for retained email OTP;
- final domain registrar/DNS/hosting.

You do **not** need to create AI, affiliate-commerce, moderation, Help Scout, PostHog, Sentry, Google OAuth, or other all-features-era accounts for lean V1 unless you later opt back into those features.

What to give back: organization/project names or IDs that are safe to share, region/plan, role granted, and redacted proof the account is active. Keep API secrets in the provider/EAS/Supabase secret store; never paste them into chat.

## H-05 — Physical iPhone actions

Some evidence cannot be created from source/web simulation. On one or more supported iPhones you (or a named tester) must perform the exact Codex-directed checklist for:

- signed build install/relaunch/background/force-stop;
- camera and real label OCR;
- progress photo capture/storage/delete/app-lock/biometric behavior where enabled;
- local notification permission and actual delivery;
- VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion and safe-area/touch behavior;
- StoreKit sandbox/TestFlight purchase, restore and manage-subscription behavior;
- final binary identity/entitlement/capability checks that require the signed archive/device.

What to give back: device model, iOS version, build/version, pass/fail notes and screenshots/video/logs that contain no private health/photo content unless intentionally sanitized.

## H-06 — Business decisions

You must approve the choices that are truly yours:

- US-first public launch (or a different exact territory);
- monthly/annual prices and whether an Apple-managed intro offer is used;
- maximum vendor/professional/launch budgets;
- tax/business decisions that require your accountant/lawyer;
- who owns support/escalation at launch;
- any explicitly documented residual risk you are willing to accept.

I can model/recommend options, but I cannot bind your company or sign for you.

## H-07 — Final release authorization

After the release packet is immutable and reviewed, you must separately authorize:

1. submission to App Review; and
2. public availability after Apple approval and the final production recheck.

The first release should remain manual until the second authorization.

## V-01 / V-02 — Apple/vendor outcomes

Apple and vendors control their own approval, review, account and limit decisions. I can prepare, submit through an authorized session, monitor, respond and fix issues, but I cannot fabricate or guarantee their outcome.

## H-08 — Genuine beta users and responses

Recruit 3-5 internal/friendly testers first, then 15-30 total target users before the first limited public-release decision. Codex prepares recruitment, TestFlight instructions, surveys, triage and fixes. Actual people must supply genuine use and feedback.
