## Build order versus blocked evidence
The numbered order prioritizes engineering work. It does not force idle time while people, devices, permissions or cohorts are unavailable. Brain may advance only to an explicitly independent task (not a dependent implementation) and must leave the blocked gate visible. Research-intake order remains fixed unless an ADR explains a real dependency, recorded research blocker or evidence change. Priority predecessors are distinct from actual measurement dependencies in roadmap.json, so a missing unrelated pack is never treated as a scientific prerequisite. Every phase ends with an evidence packet, not a calendar claim.
