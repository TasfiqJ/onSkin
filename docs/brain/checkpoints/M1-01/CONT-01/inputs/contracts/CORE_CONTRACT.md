# Unified core architecture and product contract
## Shape and boundaries
Use an incremental modular monolith. Retain the current `apps/mobile` Expo/React Native/TypeScript app, `packages/types`, Supabase/Postgres and existing verified auth/storage/payment adapters. Do not turn the catalog operator console into a practitioner workspace. Add a dedicated professional/client web surface when P4 reaches it, reusing typed contracts and existing web/build conventions where appropriate.
The directories below are logical target boundaries, not assertions that these folders already exist. Codex chooses the least-disruptive exact placement after inspecting main: domain/record, domain/formula, domain/routine, domain/rights, domain/billing, domain/professional, features/capture, features/analysis, infrastructure/native-camera, infrastructure/local-artifacts. Shared contracts have no UI or privileged credentials. Research/evaluation Python stays outside the production JS dependency graph. Avoid premature microservices, generic plugin marketplaces and an always-on GPU backend.

## Personal record is the product spine
Entities: OwnerIdentity; ConsentReceipt; ProductIdentity; FormulaVersion; OwnedProductVersion; PersonalRoutineVersion; ActualUseEvent; ExceptionEvent; CorrectionEvent; CaptureSession; AnalysisRun; RegionResult; ComparisonResult; ExportJob; DeletionTombstone. Professional entities: Workspace, Membership/Role, ScopedGrant, IntendedPlanVersion, PlanAcknowledgement, ReconciliationItem, ReviewDecision, BillingAuthority and CapacityReservation.
Each immutable entity carries ID, owner/workspace as applicable, source/provenance, schema version, creation time, actor and stable content hash. Mutable projections carry an expected revision for compare-and-swap. Event timestamps distinguish observed time, recorded time and import time; do not rewrite them when syncing. Corrections append a new event referencing the old event, not history mutation. Do not put personal content or report masks into diagnostic logs.
A product name or barcode is not a formula. Store market/variant, label text, user confirmation, source rights, parsed-vs-confirmed status, formula version and confidence basis. Unknown formula/version stays unknown; ingredient order does not prove concentration. Reformulation creates a new version, and an old bottle retains its historical version. Manual entry always works when OCR/catalog is unavailable.

## Intended versus actual is an authorization boundary
The owner controls actual routine and recorded use. A practitioner may issue an immutable intended plan within a live scoped grant, not edit the owner's actual history. Acknowledging a plan means receiving/understanding it, not automatically applying it. Applying suggestions requires an explicit owner transaction and version comparison. Silence, an unchecked task, missing photo, declining a plan or ending a relationship does not mean noncompliance or improvement. Optional exception logging is useful even without daily check-offs; distinguish “no change reported” from “affirmatively no change”.
The first professional release excludes diagnosis, prescription management, emergency monitoring and high-risk treatment automation. Educational conflict/routine/cycle recommendations continue to require the exact existing review/corpus gates. Factual ingredient/record comparison can operate while positive advice is unavailable.

## Ownership, sync and deletion
Guest-first native experience remains useful. Optional Apple/email account portability must not silently join owners. A verified account link requires proof of the same owner and explicit conflict resolution, not email similarity. Hosted client web accounts are independently controlled by the client. Practice cancellation never destroys a client's login or forces Plus.
Local images, thumbnails, masks, embeddings and raw/composite captures use the existing owner-bound encrypted artifact store and lifecycle protections. No default cloud photo backup, support upload or training. Optional text sync is separately consented and encrypted at rest; do not claim end-to-end encryption merely because TLS and disk encryption exist. Local-only photos may be unrecoverable after uninstall/device/key loss; disclose this and provide explicit owner export without promising restoration.
Withdrawal/deletion immediately stops publication, cancels outstanding jobs and invalidates owner/grant epochs. Purge active data within the reviewed policy, and ensure restore reapplies tombstones before serving traffic. Retention defaults from the V2 table (30-day active purge target, 35-day backup ageing, three-year practice-authored proposal, dormancy notices) are specifications requiring legal review and tested implementation, not verified promises. An exported file cannot be remotely revoked; disclose that limitation. Retain only lawfully required records with access/purpose restriction, never use a legal-hold placeholder to retain everything.

## Workspace and client grants
One active practice grant per owner in the first implementation. A grant lists workspace, owner, fields/purposes, current version/epoch, expiry, permissions and consent receipt. Switching practice atomically revokes the old epoch and accepts the new grant; failure leaves the transaction unchanged, never two active grants. Prior received plan history remains owner-visible without leaking it to the next practice.
Enforce authorization server-side at request time AND final publication time. Client-side flags are only UI hints. Outbox requests, exports and review actions must recheck owner/session/grant/payment authority immediately before publishing. Online professional UI must not cache health text in service workers/localStorage/IndexedDB; no-store responses and explicit logout/invalidation. Never log source text, images or bearer tokens in failures.
Roles follow the full adopted role-action table: owner/admin may administer workspace capacity and roles, practitioners act within granted clients and their permissions, assistants have least-privilege workflow rights, clients own their personal record. A workspace admin role is not universal permission to private consumer data. Deny cross-tenant IDs even when syntactically valid.

## Professional reconciliation and bounded operations
Queue states are `new`, `assigned`, `awaiting_information`, `in_review`, `awaiting_client_ack`, `resolved`, `declined_referred`, `relationship_closed`. Each transition checks actor, grant, intended-plan revision and required reason. Material start/stop/substitute/concern/clarification/formula-correction events can create items; ordinary check-offs and silence do not create urgent cases.
Deduplicate by owner event ID + version + payload hash. Reused ID with changed payload rejects. Group the same category/workspace/owner/grant-version/plan-version within 24 hours. A material event within seven days of closure reopens a new revision, preserving the closed decision. Do not mutate a resolved record to hide new work.
Initial staffing policy: expected practitioner review two business days within that practice's actual staffed window; warning when oldest unassigned exceeds one business day or backlog exceeds 10; pause new activation when oldest exceeds five business days or backlog exceeds 20 per practitioner. No emergency-response claims. Client digest at most once/day, overdue messages at most two per rolling seven days, staff digests at most twice/day. A 21-day unresolved prompt does not silently close the relationship.
Offboarding ends new paid authorship at verified authority expiry; preserve lawful read/export and the independently owned client record. Capacity is atomically reserved for accepted active plans, not mere invites: Solo20/Studio60 as experiment defaults, no hidden overage or destructive downgrade. At capacity, block new activations with a clear reason rather than delete history. Keep workflow backlog capacity separate from paid plan capacity.

## Consumer and professional billing are separate
Keep verified StoreKit/RevenueCat exact-owner ordering, restore/manage and free fallback. Never issue Plus because a local `paid=true`, a free comparison succeeded, the user joined a workspace, the provider is unknown, a receipt is stale, or a webhook replay arrived late. Three distinct free advanced operations per calendar month remain a free feature. Basic role/ingredient comparison, ordinary edits/logs/history, local photos, standard brief, data-rights export and deletion are free before/after expiry.
Meter advanced decision operations atomically using owner timezone frozen at the first operation of the month. Compare/preview/save for one confirmed candidate+base plan+cutoff decision ID count once. Aborted-before-publication or fully abstained jobs release the reservation; a published result with a lost acknowledgement must resume without double charging. Reads/exports of saved advanced output stay free. Local Plus advanced use is not an extra per-call charge.
Workspace billing uses a separate verified provider authority. Test `incomplete`, `active`, `cancel_at_period_end`, three-day past-due grace, `suspended`, `expired`, `refunded` and `disputed` cases. Signed webhook + provider retrieval, monotonic event watermarks and idempotency; refunds/chargebacks cannot be undone by an older event. Thirty-day read-only offboarding is a proposal, not a license to delete client data. Keep native workspace checkout/upsell absent pending exact Apple classification; fall back to professional web-only instead of assuming an exception.
Preserve uninterrupted current subscriber SKU/price/benefits. New hypothetical offers require explicit opt-in migration after the current term, notice and prevention of overlapping purchases. Never promise the app can cancel Apple subscriptions automatically.

## Product decisions and appointment brief
Produce deterministic, traceable comparisons from confirmed product/formula/routine data. Missing concentrations, incomplete labels and absent reviewed guidance must appear as unknown/limitations, not confident compatibility. What-if is an uncommitted scenario until owner confirmation. Product effectiveness and photo change do not establish causality from observational history.
The standard brief leads with dated owner facts, intended/actual versions, selected relevant changes, questions and reviewed explanations. Exports are user-initiated, scoped, versioned, temporary-file-cleaned and free. Local owner-selected photos may be included in a private local export; no photo sharing through the text-only professional grant, no background upload, and no paid-media gate. The broader J14/J27 architecture does not authorize free-form diagnosis.

## Reliability, support and measurements
Keep content-free operational counters separate from clinical metrics and commercial metrics. Count capture refusals/unsupported configurations, abstentions, queue latency, event conflicts and restore failures without image-bearing telemetry. Optional support review requires a separately scoped consent and secure transfer; training consent is never implicit.
Adopt the V2 table's capacity, response and retention specifications only with named funded owners and tested configurations. A 99.5% service target, RPO24h and one-business-day RTO cannot be asserted from a free project signup. Test restore quarterly and before hosted admission; apply tombstones first. If support forecast exceeds 80% funded capacity, first-response backlog exceeds two business days, or critical coverage is absent, pause onboarding before buying growth. Keep founder labour and professional time in financial reporting even when no invoice is paid.
