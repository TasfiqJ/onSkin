# Beta contract — proposal, NOT distribution authorization

## Three different milestones
1. **Internal engineering demonstration:** synthetic/test-only data in an authorized local environment. It can demonstrate the core product without pretending that providers, professional authority or J01 are genuine.
2. **Limited private consumer beta:** a separate explicitly approved cohort and exact signed build, with only qualified exposed capabilities and genuine identity/data handling wherever required. Not authorized yet.
3. **Full public release:** unchanged original all38/all-applicable-gates contract.

The frozen contract is iPhone/iOS17+, adult18+, with US/California cohort candidates and uncleared territory. Do not infer a new legal territory from the founder's current location. Do not call an internal synthetic demo a public web beta or TestFlight acceptance.

## Recommended first private-beta surface
Manual owner-entered Shelf, explicit owner routine, Today actual check-off, immutable correction/history, private text brief with review/cancel, accessible navigation and privacy rights. Include photos/reminders only after their signed-device privacy and permission behavior is qualified. No automated medical guidance or claims.

Professional positive admission, catalog-positive/OCR claims, payments, commerce, community, provider Ask, widgets and J01 measurements are excluded from this proposed first cohort unless separately qualified. Remove or visibly disable inaccessible positive paths; test route/API bypass, not merely hidden buttons. These features remain REQUIRED in the full-release roadmap. This proposal does not change the frozen release contract or waive a gate.

## Non-negotiable beta entry gates
B01: exact scope/cohort/territory/data-flow and retention approved; named accountable reviewers and support.
B02: exact candidate has real end-to-end runtime evidence, persisted reload and independently reviewed severe defects; zero unresolved P0/P1 in exposed scope.
B03: genuine owner isolation, consent, encryption/key custody, export, withdrawal and deletion qualified for that build; synthetic auth never used for real participants.
B04: signed supported iPhone install plus capture/notifications/accessibility checks for every exposed native capability; distribution authority separately approved.
B05: backend/DB qualification for every exposed server path; no placeholder success or unreviewed hosted service.
B06: security findings disposition and original custodian applicability reviewed; no rewriting historical failures to pass.
B07: kill/disable procedure, feedback, incident escalation, known limitations and participant withdrawal work.
B08: founder explicitly authorizes the exact beta artifact and cohort after independent review.

All B01–B08 begin NOT_ACCEPTED. Use a gate applicability map reviewed by the responsible original owners. Marking a full-release feature “not exposed in this beta” is not formal acceptance. If the original contract requires a gate for this beta, obtain a scoped amendment/approval before any distribution. No real participants until then.
